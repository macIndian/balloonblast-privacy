1. Introduction

Balloon Blast is an offline arcade game for Android, published by MTS Infotech ("we", "us"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the game handles and how.

This policy applies to the Balloon Blast app with the package ID com.mts.balloonblast.

2. Information we collect

Balloon Blast does not collect any information about you. In particular, the game does not collect:

  • personal information such as your name, email address or phone number;
  • device identifiers or advertising IDs;
  • location information;
  • usage statistics, analytics or crash reports;
  • any other information about you or your device.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features.

3. Information stored on your device

So that your progress is still there the next time you play, the game saves the following information on your device:

  • your best scores in the Classic and Time Challenge modes;
  • your overall best score;
  • which stages you have unlocked;
  • your best score and star rating for each stage;
  • your Sound, Music and Vibration settings.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it. It is never sent to us or to any external server. The game also keeps a technical graphics cache on your device to start faster; it contains no information about you.

4. Internet and network access

Balloon Blast does not need an internet connection. The app does not request internet access from Android and does not communicate with any external server. You can play the whole game offline.

5. Advertising and analytics

The current version of Balloon Blast contains no advertisements, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no tracking services. It contains no in-app purchases.

6. Third-party services

Balloon Blast does not integrate any third-party services that collect or share user information. The game is built with the Godot game engine and standard Android system libraries, which run entirely on your device and do not send information anywhere.

The game uses Google Play's in-app review feature, which may occasionally offer you to rate the game on Google Play without leaving it. If you rate or review, that is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the game itself never sees your rating or any information about you.

If you download Balloon Blast from Google Play, your use of Google Play is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

7. Permissions

Balloon Blast requests only one Android permission:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): for short, optional vibrations during gameplay, for example when a bomb is hit. You can turn vibration off at any time in the game's Settings.

The game does not request access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or any other personal data.

8. Data security and retention

Your game progress and settings stay on your device and are never transmitted. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Balloon Blast, so this information is not copied to backup services.

You can delete it at any time: uninstalling the game removes all of its locally stored data, and you can also clear it from Android's settings (Settings → Apps → Balloon Blast → Storage → Clear data).

9. Children's privacy

Balloon Blast does not collect personal information from anyone who plays it, including children. Because the game collects no personal information, it has no personal information about children to store, use or share.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or privacy practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this same web address, with a new "Last updated" date at the top of the page.
